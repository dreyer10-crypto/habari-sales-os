Leads shout → proposals: at the monthly shout, reps log their target clients on the Leads Shout tab (paste the list or import the CG LEADS sheet). Those sit as Leads until a proposal goes out — hit Send proposal to move a lead into the live pipeline.
Roll-overs: can't close a client this month but it's coming back? Roll over the lead to a named month with evidence (signed schedule / confirmed budget). It carries forward instead of counting as lost.
How sends work: Each "Follow-up" button opens a pre-filled draft in your default mail app (Outlook). You review and hit send — nothing leaves without you. Data is saved to this browser and persists between sessions.
Follow-up rule: flagged 5 working days after the proposal is sent (or after the last follow-up). Adjust per row when editing.
