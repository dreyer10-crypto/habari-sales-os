01
Why Wireforce

You already build the fence. This is the fixing that belongs on it.

Wireforce is the largest wire converter in sub-Saharan Africa and the only fully integrated manufacturer of high-security fencing systems in South Africa — serving construction, security, agriculture, engineering and mining. The Carbontech 4-Toe clamp does one thing: it secures wire mesh to posts. That is Wireforce's core product line, which makes this an accessory that slots directly into what you already sell — not a bolt-on from another category.

Wirewall A native fit

Wirewall is a high-security welded-mesh system (3 / 4 / 5.6 mm wire, 12.7 × 75 mm aperture) built around concealed, tamper-resistant fixings. A non-conductive FRP clamp with zero scrap value reinforces exactly that anti-tamper, anti-theft story.

Mining Your toughest environment

Wireforce supplies international mining houses and mining-support products. Mine perimeters are corrosive and electrically sensitive — the two conditions where galvanised fixings fail first and FRP wins outright.

Security Substations & live perimeters

Around substations, power plants and electrified boundaries, a non-conductive clamp is a safety feature, not just a spec — a differentiator for Wireforce's security-sector tenders.

Agri & Coastal Long life, low upkeep

Standards, droppers and stock fencing in humid, coastal and high-UV conditions last longer with a clamp that cannot rust and will not degrade in sunlight — fewer replacement cycles for the end user.

02
The deepsearch · fixing options compared

Where a material change removes several problems at once

We reviewed Wireforce's current mesh-fixing options — galvanised standards & droppers, wire ties and concealed steel fixings. They are proven and cost-effective in benign conditions. The Carbontech clamp is not a replacement for all of them; it is the premium, non-metallic tier for the jobs where steel's weaknesses cost the most.

Selection criterion Conventional galvanised-steel fixing Carbontech 4-Toe FRP clamp
Corrosion — mining / coastal / humid Zinc coating abrades and wears; rusts from cut ends and scratches Inherently corrosion-proof — no coating to fail
Electrical environments Conductive — a hazard near substations & live perimeters Non-conductive glass-reinforced nylon
Theft & vandalism Steel carries scrap value — an incentive to strip Zero scrap value — low theft incentive
Verified load Varies by gauge; rarely load-certified per fixing >230 kg failure, FEA + physical test, SF 3.31
Handling on site Heavier; more awkward to place and adjust Lightweight — single-hand fit, no special tools
Fire & heat — UL94 HB, heat-deflection >200 °C
Lifecycle cost Lower unit price, higher replacement & maintenance in harsh sites Premium unit price, offset by longer service life

Net: for Wireforce's corrosion-heavy, electrically sensitive and high-security jobs, the FRP clamp turns four separate steel liabilities — rust, conductivity, scrap-theft and un-certified load — into a single specifiable part.

03
Technical validation

Strength is not a claim — it is measured

The Carbontech 4-Toe clamp has been validated through physical load testing and finite element analysis (Autodesk Inventor, von Mises criterion). Both methods agreed on failure load and failure location.

3.31
Safety factor against a 1-in-50-year extreme wind event. At five clamps per post, a modelled 3.456 kN post load puts just 691 N on each clamp — against a tested failure load of 2,285 N. The clamp is over three times stronger than the worst weather it is likely to see.

Commercial path

A low-friction route from sample to supply

No channel-switching, no retooling of your systems. Qualify the part on your own product, prove it on one hard site, then scale it the way that suits Wireforce best.

Sample & fitment

Confirm fit on Wirewall 3 / 4 / 5.6 mm mesh and standard posts, using the same screw fixing you install today. Sample units and drawings supplied on request.

Field pilot

Deploy on one mining or coastal-security site at five clamps per post. Validate installation speed, in-service performance and end-client response over a defined window.

Supply model

Scale as a Wireforce-branded / private-label accessory SKU, or as wholesale supply into your range. Local moulding supports up to 100,000 units in ~10 days.
