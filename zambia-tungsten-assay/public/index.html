Samples & QA/QC

Readings grouped by sample label. Multiple reads → mean grade, spread (RSD) and a crushed-vs-uncrushed check. High spread flags a heterogeneous sample that needs finer prep or more reads.

Crushed vs uncrushed — accuracy check

Samples with both a raw and a prepared reading. A large gap confirms why crushing matters and tells you how far to trust field numbers.

Mineral vectoring

The element suite of each sample, read as a geologist would: which mineral you're on, and which other minerals to pursue to expand the base. Every call is an XRF inference — confirm with the UV lamp, a magnet, and lab work as flagged.
Read this like a compass, not a verdict. Bulk XRF gives diluted ore proxies, so ratios and co-occurrence matter more than any single number. The wolframite/scheelite split, and every Li / REE / gold / base-metal call, are the ones most likely to be wrong from XRF alone — route those to UV, magnet and certified-lab confirmation. Thresholds are starting points; tune them to your own background.
