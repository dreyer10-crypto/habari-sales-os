01The goal

Identify what you have — then expand the base.

Two goals, one system. Every reading should answer both.

⛏

Identify & grade-control

Know the mineral and the grade at the dig face, this shift — ore vs waste against your WO₃ cut-off, best intercepts chased, stockpiles blended to the ~65% WO₃ concentrate spec. The XRF is fast enough to run the mine, once anchored to lab assays.

◈

Expand the mineral base

Your Sn-W signature is a fertile rare-metal system. The same element suite that grades your tungsten also vectors toward cassiterite (tin), columbite-tantalite, bismuth-molybdenum and lithium-fertile ground — new revenue lines hiding in data you already collect.

The compounding asset
Every sample you log — photo + UV + XRF + magnet + eventual lab assay — becomes a row in a ground-truthed, site-specific database. Over months that is a grade-control history, a geospatial ore map, and the only training set that could ever make a camera classifier work on your rock. Start collecting from day one, before you build anything clever.

03How identity is decided

Three signals, in strict order of trust.

The camera never overrules chemistry. Make the hierarchy explicit in the app so no one acts on the weakest signal.

1

XRF chemistry authority

Elements and grade. W+Fe/Mn → wolframite; W+Ca → scheelite; Sn → cassiterite; plus every pathfinder. This is the number decisions rest on — once it's calibrated to your lab.

2

UV fluorescence + magnet confirm

Cheap, fast, near-definitive where XRF is weakest. Shortwave UV: scheelite fluoresces blue-white, wolframite is inert — and the glow shifts yellow as penalty-element Mo rises. A magnet separates magnetic wolframite from non-magnetic scheelite/cassiterite.

3

Camera + ML assist only

A suggestion with a confidence score and an honest "uncertain." Its job is triage ("worth an XRF read here") and building the dataset — never a verdict. Photo-only mineral ID is not reliable for your ores (see §5).

Two hard instrument truths baked into the engine. (1) The XL2 reads gold on a line that overlaps tungsten (Au Lα ≈ W Lβ, 40 eV apart) — so a W-rich sample manufactures phantom gold; it is suppressed and flagged, never graded. Real gold is proven only by fire assay. (2) Ca reads poorly in a W matrix, so the scheelite call leans on the UV lamp, not the chemistry. Thresholds are starting points — calibrate them to your own background and CRMs.
05The render app · layout

An offline-first field app, camera and all.

A Progressive Web App (PWA) — one codebase, installs to the phone home screen, no app store, works with no signal at the pit and syncs when it's back on the network. The camera is connective tissue, not the identifier.

Capture · device
Camera (getUserMedia, rear cam) · paired normal + UV photo of the same spot · GPS + timestamp · magnet-test toggle · voice/text note · sample ID. HTTPS is mandatory for camera access.
Identify · authority
XRF reading entered or imported from the Niton (CSV) → grade + element suite. The rule-based vectoring engine runs here: mineral call + pursue list + confirm method. Transparent, inspectable, no black box.
Assist · optional
On-device ML (TensorFlow.js / ONNX Runtime Web, MobileNet transfer-learned) returns a ranked hint with confidence and an "uncertain" state. Inference runs on the phone — never needs signal. Suggestion only.
Store · offline-first
IndexedDB holds records + queued photos; a service worker caches the app shell & model for offline. Opportunistic sync to Supabase when back online — the shared, role-controlled database you already have.
Decide · cloud
Aggregation, backup, map view, grade-control analytics, and model retraining from the growing labelled dataset. Cloud is for thinking, not field inference.

Stack, concretely

  • Shell: PWA (installable, service worker + Workbox), your existing Tungsten Assay OS design system.
  • Camera: navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia({video:{facingMode:'environment'}}) → canvas → 224×224 for the model.
  • ML: TensorFlow.js or ONNX Runtime Web; MobileNetV3 transfer learning; prototype the first classifier in Teachable Machine from your early labelled photos.
  • Data: IndexedDB (offline) → Supabase (Postgres + auth + row-level security + storage for images).
  • Target device: standardise field crews on a known Android model — far fewer web-camera / PWA restrictions than iOS, which is best-effort.
06Camera — the honest read

What the camera can and can't do.

✕

Won't work

Point-and-identify from a photo. Published models hit 85–98% on clean lab images and collapse in the field; wolframite, scheelite and cassiterite are exactly the ores whose colour/lustre are least diagnostic and whose look-alikes matter economically.

✓

Works, cheaply

UV mode for scheelite — a ~$100–300 shortwave lamp is the single smartest purchase. Plus the camera as geotagged documentation glue tying photo → UV → XRF → magnet → lab into one auditable record.

◷

Long game

An on-device ML assist that gets genuinely good only after months of your labelled samples accumulate. The payoff is a proprietary dataset, not a magic identifier you install on day one.

The one-liner for the capex committee: the camera app's job is to document and organise; the XRF is the identifier; the $200 UV lamp is the smartest single purchase for scheelite; and the machine-learning payoff is a dataset you build over years, not a product you buy today.
07Build sequence

Phased roadmap — value first, risk deferred.

Phase 1 · build now · weeks

The useful core

Everything that delivers operational value with essentially zero ML risk.

  • The assay database + grade dashboard + mineral vectoring — already built.
  • Multi-user backend with logins & roles (Supabase) — already built, awaiting your project keys.
  • PWA wrapper: installable, offline (service worker + IndexedDB), camera capture with GPS + timestamp.
  • UV photo mode (paired normal + shaded UV shot) + magnet-test field feeding the wolframite/scheelite call.
  • Buy the shortwave UV lamp + safety glasses and a magnet / susceptibility pen.
Phase 2 · 3–6 months

Dataset + assist model

  • Accumulate labelled records (photo + UV + XRF + magnet + lab ground-truth field).
  • Once a few hundred+ of your images exist, train a MobileNet suggestion-only classifier, on-device, with confidence + "uncertain".
  • UV-colour → Mo-penalty warning; map view of samples; grade-control reconciliation.
Phase 3 · optional capex

Only if justified

  • Handheld Raman for unambiguous single-grain mineral confirmation (an identity channel to complement XRF chemistry).
  • Cloud retraining pipeline + analytics at scale.
Don't bother / actively skip: consumer rock-ID apps as an operational tool (collector-tuned, unvalidated on ore); SWIR/hyperspectral bought to identify ore (it's an alteration/exploration tool, weak on your actual minerals); any design that puts the photo classifier above the chemistry or needs signal for field inference; and longwave "blacklight" LEDs for scheelite — get proper shortwave UV.
