Delivery over time

Daily impressions, July 2026

Ad server

The flight opened slowly. The first five days averaged 5,966 impressions a day and contributed 6.5% of the month's total. From 6 July delivery settled into a 10,000–34,000 band, averaging 16,450 a day for the balance of the month. The single highest day was 11 July at 33,776.

Daily clicks, July 2026

Ad server

Engagement follows the same shape. The opening five days returned 3 clicks between them — under 1% of the month's total. Across the remaining 26 days the flight ran at a 0.079% CTR, with the strongest single day on 30 July at 0.126%.

Analysis

What July tells us

6.5%

The ramp cost real volume

The first five days carried 6.5% of impressions and 0.9% of clicks. Getting a flight to full pace on day one is worth roughly 52,000 impressions on a month this size.

33,776

Fixtures drive the traffic

The month's biggest day was a Test Saturday, and the third biggest was the next one. A quiet Saturday with no rugby returned 10,486. The calendar, not the creative, sets the ceiling.

92%

One unit did the work

The 300×250 drove 92% of all clicks off 66% of impressions. The 320×50 returned 8 clicks on 50,375 impressions, and the 728×90 could not secure inventory at volume.
