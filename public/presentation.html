Build 05 · never lose a flight

Lead classes & rebooks.

Short-term vs long-term

Every lead is classed. It flows straight into the proposal.

  • ST buys monthly / bi-monthly · single-flight proposal · quicker to close
  • LT bigger budget · books 3 / 6 / 12 months · multi-month proposal prefilled
  • Big advertisers auto-classed long-term

🔁 Rebooks — ran & ended

A flight that ran (e.g. July) and has nothing booked next becomes a rebook — still that rep's to re-close.

  • Rep has 2 months to re-close · Due / Lapsed views
  • Not closed in time → lapses → manager reassigns to a different rep
  • “Different voice, different day brings more deals.”

Rebooks are opened from the lead drawer (＋ Booking ran → e.g. “JULY 2026”). Signed bookings via Integrations open the rebook clock automatically.

Build 09 · integrations & compliance

Signed bookings in, Outlook matched, POPIA by design.

🧾

Booking intake

Log a signed booking (client, rep, publication, period, value, class) — it assigns the lead, marks it won, and starts the rebook clock. CSV import + secure webhook for e-sign tools.

📧

Outlook / M365

Each rep connects their own mailbox (Mail.Read). The system matches emails to leads and logs replies as touches — it never sends.

🛡️

POPIA by design

Voice-first — reps call; the system never emails a bought list. Mailbox access needs the rep's consent + Dreyer / counsel sign-off.

Booking webhook enabled with a WEBHOOK_KEY; Outlook via a free Azure AD app (Mail.Read). No auto-send anywhere in the system.
