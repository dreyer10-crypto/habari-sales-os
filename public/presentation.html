The problem

Leads live in a WhatsApp shout and an Excel file. Proposals sit in inboxes. Follow-ups depend on who remembers. Managers have no live view of the pipeline — and no simple way to see who's actually closing.

  • Leads get lost between the shout and the proposal.
  • Follow-ups slip — no clock, no flag, no nudge.
  • “Coming next month” deals quietly vanish.
  • No live scoreboard — effort isn't visible or rewarded.

01 · Lead generation

The Leads Shout — captured, not scattered.

When the monthly shout goes out, each rep lists the clients they'll chase. Three ways in — all in the browser, no credentials.

  • Import the CG LEADS sheet — pick the .xlsx straight from Drive/SharePoint download; the title row is skipped, clients land tagged to the month.
  • Paste a list — one client per line, or full columns copied from Excel.
  • Type them in — quick add, one at a time.

Publication is guessed from the sheet name (SARW → SA Rugby Magazine). A lead sits with no follow-up clock until a proposal goes out.

Never lose a live client

Roll-overs — carried forward, with evidence.

A client that won't close this month isn't a loss if it's genuinely coming back. The rep rolls it to a named month and attaches the proof.

  • Evidence required — signed schedule, confirmed budget, email on file.
  • Carries forward as a lead for the month named, not a “Lost”.
  • Manager sees it — every roll-over and its evidence on the dashboard.
