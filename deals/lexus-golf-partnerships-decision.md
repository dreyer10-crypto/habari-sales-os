# Lexus — Golf Partnerships Decision (Decomposition)

> **Confidential — internal Habari deal note.** Not part of the deployed site (`public/`). Do not forward.
> Source: client feedback email received post internal EXCO alignment. Logged 2 Aug 2026.

## 1. Headline decision

**Full decline of all proposed golf partnerships — for now, not forever.**

- Every golf partnership proposal Habari put forward is being **excluded from current plans**.
- Lexus will redirect the available **time and budget** to activities that are **already approved**.
- This is a **"not now," not a "never."** The relationship and the door are explicitly kept open.

| # | Reason | In their words |

| 1 | **Time & budget** are committed elsewhere | "focusing instead on the remaining approved activities given the time and budget available" |
| 2 | **LIV Golf SA is uncertain** | "still in the process of securing funding and its long-term status for 2027 has not yet been confirmed" |
| 3 | **Can't commit to a broad golf strategy** until the anchor is certain | "difficult for us to commit to a broader golf strategy until there is greater certainty" |
| 4 | **No spend on "ancillary" golf** while the overall golf question is open | "do not believe it is the right time to continue investing in ancillary golf activities or media opportunities while our future involvement… remains undecided" |

## 3. The subtext (read between the lines)

- **LIV Golf SA is the anchor asset.** In their mental model everything Habari pitched is *ancillary* / *supporting* to LIV. Their whole golf decision is **gated on LIV**, not on our proposals.
- **The decline is about timing and certainty — not quality or relationship.** They praised the effort **twice** ("time, effort and thought"; "we appreciate the effort"). Nothing in the email criticises the proposals themselves.
- **This cycle's budget is already spoken for.** New golf spend is off the table for the current period regardless of merit.
- **They want an *integrated ecosystem*, not a shopping list.** Future interest is framed as "supporting properties and media channels… part of a more integrated golf ecosystem" — i.e. anchored to LIV, not standalone buys.

## 4. Conditions to revisit (the openings they left)

They named the exact triggers that would reopen this:

1. **A suitable LIV Golf partnership opportunity** presents itself.
2. **Greater certainty on LIV Golf SA** — funding secured and **2027 status confirmed**.
3. **A stronger business case** can be established.
4. Framed as a **single integrated golf ecosystem** (LIV anchor + supporting properties + media), not ancillary line items.

## 5. Recommended next actions (Habari desk)

1. **Protect the goodwill.** Reply graciously and briefly — thank them, respect the decision, restate that we'll stay close to how the property develops. (Any outbound to follow PI house style.)
2. **Reposition the future pitch.** Stop selling golf ancillaries standalone. When we come back, lead with the **integrated LIV ecosystem** they described, with a hard business case.
3. **Stand up a LIV Golf SA watch.** Track the two trigger events — **funding secured** and **2027 confirmation** — and treat either as the signal to re-engage.
4. **Update the pipeline: roll over, don't mark lost.** In the Proposal Tracker, move the golf proposals to a **roll-over** with the evidence = "Lexus EXCO decline — revisit on LIV funding/2027 confirmation." This keeps them alive against a milestone instead of counting them as lost.
5. **Redirect this cycle's sell** to the **approved activities** they *are* funding — capture that budget now.
6. **Diarise the check-in against LIV milestones**, not a generic 5-day follow-up. Re-engage when the trigger fires, not before.

## 6. One-line summary

> Lexus has parked **all** golf partnerships this cycle on **timing + LIV uncertainty**, not on proposal quality — the relationship is warm and the door reopens the moment **LIV Golf SA's funding and 2027 status are confirmed** and we can pitch **one integrated golf ecosystem** with a strong business case.
