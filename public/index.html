Project 02

The Proposal Tracker

One screen for the whole sales cycle. It starts at the monthly leads shout: on the Leads Shout tab each rep lists the clients they'll chase this month — pasted in or imported straight from the CG LEADS sheet. Those sit as leads until a proposal goes out; one click turns a lead into a tracked proposal with a follow-up flag five working days out and a one-click Habari advertising agreement. Can't land a client this month? Roll it over to a named month with evidence and it carries forward instead of counting as lost. Every entry feeds the Sales Dashboard below. It's live here — try the Leads Shout tab.
