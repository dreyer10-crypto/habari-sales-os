Escalation ladder: a lead is flagged when it stays open (unbooked, or not followed up) past the 30-day window → Manager to act. If the manager doesn't act (nudge / escalate / reassign) within the 5-day SLA, or the lead is still open past 45 days, it escalates to the Sales Director.
Nudge rep writes a prompt into the lead that the rep sees in their tracker (and opens an email). View leads opens that rep's tracker. Return to pool un-assigns the lead so another rep can claim it.
Leads & roll-overs: the Leads KPI and funnel stage count clients from the monthly leads shout that haven't had a proposal sent yet (estimated value shown separately from the open pipeline). Roll-overs are clients a rep has carried to a future month against supplied evidence — listed above with that evidence; they sit as next-month leads rather than counting as lost.
